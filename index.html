Straight answers

Questions we get every week

How much is this going to cost me?

It depends on the size and pitch of your roof, the material you choose, and how much of the decking underneath needs replacing — which nobody can tell you honestly over the phone. That's why the inspection and the written, itemized estimate are free. You'll see the number before you commit to anything.

Are you licensed and insured?

Yes — R.L. Hayes Roofing & Repairs is licensed, bonded and insured in Georgia, and our crews are GAF-trained. We carry warranties on both labor and materials, and you get all the manufacturer paperwork when the job is done.

How fast can somebody actually get out here?

Quickly. Free inspections are usually scheduled within a few days, and genuine emergencies — active leaks, storm damage, exposed decking — get priority. Call the number on this page and we'll tell you the soonest we can be there.

What about the mess — nails and debris in my yard?

Cleanup is part of the job. We clear the property and do a final walkthrough with you before we leave, so you can point out anything you're not happy with while we're still there.

How long does a roof replacement take?

Most homes we replace are finished in a day or two. We schedule it at a time that works for your household and give you a clear start and end date up front.

Do you offer financing?

Yes. We're a GreenSky® merchant, so you can break a roofing project into monthly payments instead of paying it all at once. Checking your options doesn't affect your credit. Ask us when we come out.

My roof was damaged in a storm — do you deal with insurance?

We do this constantly. Call us before you call your insurance company if you can: we'll inspect and photograph the damage in the format adjusters need, before anything gets disturbed, then work with your adjuster directly.
