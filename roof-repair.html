Straight answers

Repair questions we hear every week

How fast can you get here?

Emergencies get priority — active leaks, storm damage and exposed decking go to the front of the line. Regular free inspections are usually scheduled within a few days. Call and we'll tell you the soonest we can be out.

Is the inspection really free?

Yes. No cost, no obligation, and no sales pressure. We'll tell you what your roof actually needs — even if the honest answer is "nothing right now."

Will you try to sell me a whole new roof?

No. If a repair will do the job, we'll say so. We'd rather do your repair now and your replacement in ten years than lose you as a customer over one oversold job.

My roof was damaged in a storm — will insurance cover it?

Often, yes. Call us before you file if you can. We inspect and photograph all the damage in the format adjusters need, before anything is disturbed, then work directly with your adjuster for a fair settlement.

Are you licensed and insured?

Licensed, bonded and insured in Georgia, with GAF-trained crews and warranties on labor and materials.

What about the mess afterwards?

Cleanup is part of the job. We clear the property and walk the finished work with you before we leave, so you can flag anything you're not happy with while we're still there.
