Before you spend the money

Roof replacement questions, answered straight

How much does a new roof cost in Augusta?

Anyone who quotes you a number over the phone is guessing. The price depends on your roof's size and pitch, the material you pick, how many layers are coming off, and how much decking underneath needs replacing. We inspect it, then hand you an itemized written estimate for free — so you're comparing a real number, not a range.

Do I actually need a full replacement, or will a repair do?

Often a repair is enough, and we'll say so. You're generally looking at replacement when shingles are curling, cracking or missing across multiple areas, you're finding granules in your gutters, leaks keep coming back after repairs, or there's visible sagging. We'll show you what we find on your roof and let you decide.

How long will my house be a construction site?

Most homes we replace are done in a day or two. You'll get a clear start and end date up front, and we schedule it around your household.

What financing do you offer?

We're a GreenSky® merchant, with multiple repayment terms and competitive rates so you can pick a monthly payment that fits. Checking your options doesn't affect your credit. Subject to credit approval.

What if you find rotten decking after the tear-off?

It happens, and it's the one thing nobody can see until the old roof comes off. That's why decking replacement is priced on your estimate up front — so if we find it, you already know exactly what it costs. No surprise invoices.

What warranty comes with the roof?

Warranties on both labor and materials. We hand over all the manufacturer paperwork when the job is finished so your investment is documented and protected.

Will my insurance pay for it?

If the damage came from a storm, often yes. Call us before you file if you can — we'll inspect and photograph everything in the format adjusters need, then work directly with your adjuster to get a fair settlement.
